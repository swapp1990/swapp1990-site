Privacy Policy

Swapp1990 accounts: one sign-in for the apps I build.

Last updated 29 September 2026 · swapp1990.org

Who this covers

A Swapp1990 account is the shared sign-in used by the apps below. It is run by me, Swapnil Sawant, on servers I operate. This page covers the account and sign-in itself. Each app's own policy covers what that app does after you sign in.

  • DesignForYou privacy policy
  • WriteForYou privacy policy
  • LetMeActForYou privacy policy
  • SnapForYou is covered by this page.

What we receive when you sign in

  • Sign in with Google: your Google account ID, name, email address, and profile photo. We ask only for the basic openid, profile, and email scopes. We do not get your password, contacts, files, or any other Google data.
  • Sign in with Apple: your Apple user ID, and the name and email Apple shares, which may be a private relay address.
  • Email sign-in: your email address and the one-time codes we send to it.

How we use it

Only to create your account, sign you in, show your name and photo inside the apps, keep your work and purchases attached to you, and contact you about your account. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the hosting and email providers that run the service.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored in the sign-in service at auth.swapp1990.org and in each app's account records, on servers I operate. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS.

Deleting your data

Use the delete-account option in the app you use (for example LetMeActForYou), or email swapp19902@gmail.com and I will delete your account and its sign-in data. You can also remove access at any time from your Google account permissions or your Apple ID settings.

Questions or requests: swapp19902@gmail.com
