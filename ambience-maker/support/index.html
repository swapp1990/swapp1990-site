Ambience Maker

Layered soundscapes for sleep, reading and focus.

Privacy Policy · swapp1990.org

What it is

Each scene is a painted place with its own sounds: rain on a cafe window, a night train, a lighthouse in a storm. Every sound is a separate layer you can turn up or down, and some layers can be swapped, for example light rain for a storm.

Help

Changing scenes: swipe up or down. Tap the picture to show the controls; they hide again after a few seconds.

Mixing: tap Mix to set each layer's volume. Where a layer has options, swipe it left or right. Your mix is kept for every scene.

Saving: tap the heart to keep a scene in the Saved shelf of the Library.

Sleep timer: tap Timer and pick a length; the sound fades out when it ends.

Listening with the screen off: the sound keeps playing in the background and can be paused from the lock screen or Control Center.

Requesting a scene: describe a place in the Request tab. The most-requested places become new scenes in app updates.
